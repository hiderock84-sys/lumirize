03 / CONTINUOUS SUPPORTOngoing support

After moving in,
we are still here.

From housing to everyday questions,
we support daily life with respect for dignity.

01 — DAILY LIFE

Daily-life advice & regular meetings

Through meetings at 3 months, 6 months, 1 year and 2 years after moving in, we work through everyday difficulties and worries together. We protect the privacy of consultations.

02 — WORK & SOCIETY

Employment & community life

Working with employment support agencies, we help with job searches, interview and document preparation, and settling into work.

03 — PARTNERSHIP

Healthcare, welfare & justice partners

We work with welfare offices, medical providers and others to connect people with the right services and programmes, including practical help with applications.

04 — INDEPENDENCE

Skills for independent living

We help build everyday habits, budgeting, health management and communication skills for living in your own way.

05 / QUESTIONS

FAQs

Why Housing First?+

Because a safe home makes it possible to rebuild daily life. We see housing as the foundation for recovery and a new beginning.

Can I ask for help without a guarantor?+

Yes. We will listen to your situation and work through the available options and necessary procedures together.

What if I urgently need somewhere to live?+

Please call us first. Phone hours (Japan time): Mon–Fri 10:00–17:00; Sat and public holidays 10:00–13:00; closed Sundays. We will check your situation and availability and explain possible next steps.

How much do consultation and housing cost?+

Our consultation and accompaniment support cost ¥0 per session or hour. Travel and other actual expenses are explained in advance. Rent and initial costs for general housing depend on the agreement with the landlord or other provider. Night care house fees and settlement termsJapanese are explained in our operating rules.
