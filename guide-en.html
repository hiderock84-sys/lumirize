Housing support, fees & moving in

A practical guide to LUMIRIZE housing consultations. The fees below follow the operating rules effective 1 October 2026. Individual conditions are confirmed before a contract is signed.

Housing and daily-life supportFees & eligibilityFive steps to moving inBefore contacting usContact & visits

Housing and daily-life support

We welcome housing enquiries from individuals, families and support organisations in Tokyo, Kanagawa and Chiba. You can ask for help even if you do not attend Sagamihara DARC. Night care housing is primarily for Sagamihara DARC participants; vacancies, shared-living arrangements and support needs are checked individually. A 24-hour staff rotation provides daily-life consultation, monitoring and emergency response for residents. This is separate from the public enquiry hours below.

Fees & eligibility

Consultation and accompaniment support cost ¥0. Rent and initial costs for ordinary rental housing depend on the landlord’s contract. Transport and other actual expenses are explained in advance. The following standard fees apply to night care housing.

Housing¥41,000/month or ¥1,500/day. The method is agreed in the contract; both are not charged for the same period.
Common charges¥3,000/month
Utilities¥15,000/month
Basic monthly total¥59,000 (housing + common charges + utilities)
Meals¥600 per meal actually provided. Meal use is agreed when signing; prepaid charges for meals not provided are settled and refunded.
Consultation / accompaniment¥0; agreed actual expenses are separate.
Security deposit¥0; restoration costs at move-out are checked separately under the rules and contract.

Example: 30 days with 3 meals per day = ¥59,000 + (¥600 × 90 meals) = ¥113,000. Transport and other actual expenses are not included.

Personal living money is separate

The ¥60,000 monthly amount for personal spending and living expenses is the resident’s own money, not a LUMIRIZE service fee. Its funding, deposit and payment arrangements are agreed individually. When held by the company, it is kept separate from fees and company funds, with a monthly statement. Unpaid balances are carried forward or returned under the rules. If ¥60,000 is deposited in addition to the example above, the total funds are ¥173,000; not all of this is a company fee.

Before moving in, we confirm the desired area and date, availability, shared-living rules, support and medical needs, meals, language, payment and move-out conditions. Existing contracts are changed only after the necessary explanation and agreement. See the Japanese rules for prorating, absence and hospitalisation.

Five steps to moving in

  1. Free initial consultation by phone, email or an arranged visit. Anonymous enquiries are welcome.
  2. Check available public support and prepare documents; you can start before all documents are ready.
  3. View a property and discuss daily life, rent, healthcare and travel to work.
  4. Application review and contract explanation. The usual review estimate is 3–7 business days; timing varies. Same-day admission is not guaranteed.
  5. Move in and continue support, including meetings at 3 months, 6 months, 1 year and 2 years.

Before contacting us

Can I ask without a guarantor or complete documents?

Yes. We explain options and the documents needed. Admission still depends on the conditions of the property.

Which languages can I use?

Japanese, English, Korean, Tagalog and Thai. Tell us your preferred language and time; staff and interpreting arrangements are coordinated. You can write in your own language in the form.

Can I ask not to be called?

Yes. Choose email only, phone only, either method, or no reply in the form. You can also specify voicemail permission and contact times.

Are the videos real testimonials?

The four stories use fictional characters to explain support. Audio is Japanese; stories 3 and 4 offer Japanese or English subtitle editions.
